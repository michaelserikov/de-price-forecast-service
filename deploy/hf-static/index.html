German day-ahead power price forecast

Hourly 10%, 50% and 90% quantile forecasts of DE-LU day-ahead prices from an ensemble of PyTorch networks (MLP and LSTM), produced every evening by a scheduled job on public SMARD and fuel price data. Research and engineering demonstration, not investment advice. Code and method: github.com/michaelserikov/de-price-forecast-service
